## Positioning Summary
**AI Workforce Map's unique value proposition:** The only tool that assesses individual employees for specific AI-adjacent roles (Operator, Approver, Workflow Translator, QA/Risk Reviewer, Change Champion) using scenario-based assessments that measure behavioral judgment in realistic AI workflow situations.

**No existing competitor** combines:
1. AI-specific content (not generic personality/cognitive)
2. Scenario-based format (not MCQ, games, or trait questionnaires)
3. Role-specific output (not just a profile or score)
4. Internal talent development focus (not just pre-hire)
5. Alignment with DOL/consulting frameworks

**Closest competitive threats to monitor:**
- SHL (broadest capability + SJT expertise)
- Harver/Pymetrics (neuroscience + internal mobility)
- Consulting firms productizing frameworks (Deloitte, BCG)

---

## Gap Analysis: The White Space
No tool in the market specifically assesses individual employees for AI-adjacent workflow roles. The landscape:
1. **Generic talent assessment tools** (Harver, Plum, PI, Wonderlic, SHL, Arctic Shores) — measure personality, cognitive ability, motivation. Not AI-specific.
2. **Technical AI assessment tools** (TestGorilla AI test) — measure ML/AI engineering skills. Not for business users.
3. **Organizational AI readiness platforms** (Microsoft, Cisco, Bain, Mercer) — assess company-level maturity. Not individual employees.
4. **AI training platforms** — deliver AI literacy training but don't assess role-specific capability.

**AI Workforce Map uniquely occupies the white space** — assessing individual employees' behavioral judgment for specific AI-adjacent roles through scenario-based methodology.
